Getting Started

Creating Your First Lesson

  1. Open the MemQ app and tap the "Create" button
  2. Choose a learning mode, then add cards manually or generate them from a topic, text, or PDF
  3. Review your cards and tap "Save"
  4. Name your lesson and optionally add a description or deadline
  5. Save the lesson to your library

Adding Terms to Your Lesson

There are several ways to add terms:

  • Manual Entry: Tap "Add Term" and enter the term and definition yourself
  • From a topic or text: Enter what you want to learn and generate cards
  • PDF upload: Add a PDF and generate cards from its contents

Studying with Flashcards

  1. Tap "Start Learning" on Today, or start practice from a lesson
  2. Review the term on the front of the card
  3. Tap the card to flip it and see the definition
  4. Swipe right if you remember it, or left if you need more review
  5. The app will schedule future reviews based on spaced repetition
